# WP3 Search for Unrecorded Liabilities — FY2025

Prepared by: S. Agrawal  
Date: September 16, 2026

## Population and selection

The January 2026 disbursement population contains 58 payments totaling $2,431,818.64 and ties to the normalized disbursement data without difference. The $12,000 testing threshold equals clearly trivial. All 53 payments at or above the threshold were tested. The five smaller payments were also reviewed because their descriptions identified recurring services that could relate to December.

## Procedure

Each payment was evaluated based on when the underlying goods or services were received—not merely the payment date. Vendor invoice numbers and amounts were compared with the December 31 A/P listing. For generic inventory purchases already recorded in A/P, the workpaper identifies the vendor invoice date and A/P listing as the evidence used; receiving reports were not provided. This limitation does not change the unrecorded-liability result because every generic pre-year-end inventory purchase was already recorded. For the nine inventory payments absent from A/P, the disbursement descriptions explicitly state "January receipt" and the invoices are dated in January. Payments for January rent, benefits, labor, support, or January inventory receipts were correctly excluded from the December 31 liability balance.

## Exceptions

Two payments related to FY2025 services but were not on the December 31 A/P listing:

- ACH5033 / MID-55837 — Midstate Freight Lines — December 2025 outbound freight — $88,519.11.
- ACH5034 / NIT-2611 — Northpoint IT Services — December 2025 managed IT support — $3,903.09.

Total unrecorded liabilities are $92,422.20. The Midstate item is consistent with management’s statement that the carrier bills monthly in arrears and is especially relevant because management also described the December close as rushed. The Northpoint item was below the selection threshold but was identified through review of the complete service-payment population.

Proposed adjustment:

- Debit freight expense: $88,519.11.
- Debit managed IT expense: $3,903.09.
- Credit A/P or accrued liabilities: $92,422.20.

## Conclusion

The unrecorded amount of $92,422.20 is below performance materiality of $167,000 but above clearly trivial of $12,000. It is a factual misstatement and is carried to Findings as F-06. Receiving reports were not supplied for generic inventory purchases, but no such item was absent from the A/P listing; the limitation therefore does not affect the quantified exception. Combined with the existing findings, the aggregate identified misstatement is $1,095,267.87, which exceeds overall and performance materiality. Management should record the adjustment, and the audit team should consider extending the search because the missed freight accrual and rushed close indicate elevated completeness risk.
