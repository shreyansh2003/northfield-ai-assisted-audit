## Population and tie-out

- The A/R aging contains 350 open items totaling $4,737,696.16.
- The aging cross-foots and agrees to trial-balance account 1100 with no difference.
- Balances over 90 days total $865,971.87, or 18.3% of gross A/R.

- The unmatched customer balance is $642,000.00.

## Subsequent receipts population

Cash receipts through February 27, 2026 were evaluated. Direct invoice references support 440 receipt rows. All 17 “see remit” rows uniquely match an open item using customer ID and exact amount. In total, 334 of 350 open items, representing $3,870,931.74, were fully collected. Sixteen items totaling $866,764.42 had no subsequent receipt.

The unreceived population consists of:

- INV-252650, Keystone Facilities Group — $642,000.00. This item is addressed by the proposed revenue and A/R reversal in F-02 and is not counted again as an allowance adjustment.
- Ten Delmar Janitorial Services LLC invoices — $154,514.05. Every invoice is over 120 days old, the customer is on credit hold, and no receipt was identified through February 27. A 100% specific reserve is required.
- Four small over-120-day balances for Bayview, Pinnacle, Parma, and Twinsburg — $13,430.20 in total. Those customers collected between 93.3% and 99.1% of their year-end balances and are not on credit hold. The residuals are covered by the general reserve and should remain on the collection watch list.
- INV-253100 — $56,820.17. This is the duplicate posting already proposed for reversal in F-04 and is not counted again.

## Sample design

A 24-item, nonstatistical targeted selection was used:

- 100% of items at or above performance materiality.
- 100% of balances over 90 days, which also captures every credit-hold and unmatched-customer item.
- The two largest remaining current balances.

The sample totals $1,036,239.55, or 21.9% of gross A/R. Subsequent receipts were agreed by invoice reference for received items. Every unreceived item received a documented customer-level alternative procedure and conclusion. Because selection was targeted, results are not projected statistically.

## Allowance evaluation

Management’s recorded allowance is $47,400, approximately 1% of gross A/R. The flat rate does not appropriately capture the Delmar credit deterioration.

The required allowance is:

- Delmar specific reserve: $154,514.05 at 100%.
- Keystone: excluded from the allowance calculation because F-02 proposes a full receivable reversal. If F-02 is not recorded, a 100% reserve would be required.
- General reserve: 1% of $3,884,361.94 after excluding Delmar and the proposed F-02 and F-04 corrections, or $38,843.62.
- Required allowance: $193,357.67.
- Recorded allowance: $47,400.00.
- Understatement: $145,957.67.

The general reserve rate is retained because 98.2% of the remaining general-reserve base was collected by February 27, the four other old residual items are small and their customers show strong collections, and a reserve remains appropriate for unidentified loss risk.

## Conclusion

The aging is complete and accurate relative to the trial balance. A/R existence is not supported for the Keystone item already reported in F-02. The allowance is understated by $145,957.67 because Delmar requires a specific reserve; this is a judgmental misstatement above clearly trivial but below performance materiality. Combined with the existing revenue findings, net A/R is materially misstated until management records the proposed adjustments.
