# Planning memorandum

**Client:** Northfield Supply Co. (fictional)  
**Period:** Year ended December 31, 2025  
**Prepared by:** Shreyansh Agrawal  
**Date:** September 16, 2026

Northfield Supply Co. is a Cleveland-based wholesale distributor of janitorial and facility supplies. Net sales increased 8.6% to $39.9 million, compared with management’s representation that sales volume grew approximately 6%–7% and that a March price increase had only a small net effect. Gross margin increased from 25.2% to 26.7%, while income before taxes rose 124.3% to $1.7 million. A/R days decreased from 45.3 to 43.4. These results make revenue growth directionally plausible but do not, by themselves, provide sufficient evidence regarding occurrence or cutoff.

Overall materiality is $239,000, based on 0.6% of net sales. Net sales is more representative of the scale of this distribution business than pretax income, which is comparatively small and increased 124.3% year over year. Performance materiality is set at 70% of overall materiality, or $167,000, reflecting the first-year engagement, the controller’s description of a rushed December close, the presumed fraud risk in revenue recognition, and identified data-quality exceptions. The clearly trivial threshold is $12,000, or 5% of overall materiality.

The principal risks are improper revenue recognition, A/R valuation, completeness of liabilities, and the reliability of client-prepared data. Revenue recognition is treated as a presumed fraud risk and will be addressed through monthly analytics, shipping-document cutoff testing, full-population duplicate procedures, and subsequent-receipt work. The flat 1% allowance will be challenged using aging, credit-hold status, subsequent receipts, and specific-reserve evidence. The search for unrecorded liabilities will focus on January disbursements and the goods-or-services period, particularly freight billed in arrears. Client-prepared information has been normalized and reconciled to the trial balance, but the missing ship date and the customer absent from the master file require substantive follow-up.
