# WP1 Revenue Analysis — FY2025

Prepared by: S. Agrawal  
Date: September 16, 2026

## Scope and materiality

- Net sales population: 3,581 rows totaling $39,862,443.61; agrees to the Lead sheet.
- Overall materiality: $239,000.
- Performance materiality: $167,000.
- Monthly investigation threshold: $84,000, or approximately 50% of performance materiality.

## Monthly analytics

The initial expectation applied 6.5% growth to each 2024 month, preserving prior-year seasonality. Management stated that volume grew 6%–7% and that a March list-price increase had only a small net effect because many customers were protected. No operational volume report, contract schedule, or approved price-change analysis was included in the PBC. COGS growth and gross A/R days are internal financial relationships and provide only directional consistency. The expectation is therefore a risk-screening tool, not a sufficiently precise substantive analytical procedure. Audit assurance comes from investigation of flagged months, full-population duplicate testing, and shipping-document cutoff and completeness testing.

Three months exceeded the $84,000 investigation threshold:

- September: recorded revenue exceeded expectation by $583,802.54. The difference is driven by INV-252650 for $642,000. Excluding that entry, September is $58,197.46 below expectation, within threshold.
- November: recorded revenue exceeded expectation by $86,572.08. The ledger contains a duplicate posting of INV-253100 for $56,820.17. Excluding one copy, November is $29,751.91 above expectation, within threshold.
- December: recorded revenue exceeded expectation by $180,843.35. Three invoices totaling $158,067.83 were shipped in January 2026. Removing them leaves December $22,775.52 above expectation, within threshold.

## Exception INV-252650

INV-252650 for $642,000 was recorded on September 30 for Keystone Facilities Group. The customer is not in the approved customer master, the ledger has no ship date, no shipping document is present, the invoice remains entirely in the over-90-day A/R bucket at December 31, and no subsequent receipt was identified. The memo reads “Q-end - per J. Marsh,” while management represented that there were no unusual or one-time transactions. These facts indicate possible management override or fraudulent revenue recognition. Escalate to the engagement partner and those charged with governance. Obtain the contract, purchase order, shipping and delivery evidence, customer acceptance, and external confirmation; absent persuasive evidence, reverse the entry.

## Cutoff test

Selected 100% of FY2025 sales-ledger invoices dated December 23–31 with amounts of at least $15,000: 15 invoices totaling $434,697.91, representing 12.7% of December revenue. The threshold targeted individually significant items closest to year-end. Each ledger ship date was agreed to a separate shipping document.

Three invoices were shipped after December 31 and belong in FY2026:

- INV-253542 — $55,047.54 — shipped January 2, 2026.
- INV-253559 — $49,833.51 — shipped January 5, 2026.
- INV-253567 — $53,186.78 — shipped January 6, 2026.

Total cutoff overstatement: $158,067.83.

As an alternative completeness procedure, all 83 shipping documents in `D_Shipping` dated December 22, 2025 through January 6, 2026 were traced to `D_Sales`, and the document ship dates were compared with the ledger. All 83 invoice numbers were present and all ship dates agreed. Eighty documents were dated in December. Three were dated in January, and all three were the FY2025 cutoff exceptions listed above. No shipping document in the supplied window was omitted from the sales ledger. The January 2026 sales register was not provided, so this procedure covers the complete supplied shipping-document population rather than any shipments missing from that population.

## Duplicate test

The complete sales population was tested for repeated invoice numbers and for identical customer, amount, and invoice date combinations. INV-253100 appears twice with identical invoice date, ship date, customer, amount, and PO reference. One posting is a duplicate and overstates revenue and A/R by $56,820.17. No other exceptions met either duplicate criterion.

## Conclusion

WP1 identified three factual revenue overstatements totaling $856,888.00. This exceeds overall and performance materiality. The full supplied shipping-document cutoff population did not identify an additional omitted sale, but the revenue expectation remains directional and the PBC does not include a January sales register. Management should record the proposed adjustments. INV-252650 is individually material and presents a possible fraud or management-override indicator; it requires partner and governance escalation and expanded procedures before the audit team can conclude that revenue is fairly stated.
